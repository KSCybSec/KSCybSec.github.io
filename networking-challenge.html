NETWORKING PATH · CHALLENGE

Trace the connection.

One laptop. One network. A few clues. Can you follow the path from the device to the server?

THE SCENARIO

Something is connecting.

A laptop has just joined a network. It received an IP address and is trying to reach a secure website.

You have been given a few pieces of evidence. Your job is not to memorize the answers.

Follow the clues and figure out what happens at each step.

INVESTIGATION

Connect the clues.

Choose the answer that best explains what is happening. You can change your answer before submitting.

CLUE · 01

What protocol gave the laptop its network configuration?

DHCP automatically provides configuration such as an IP address, subnet mask, gateway and DNS server.
CLUE · 02

The laptop wants to send traffic to 192.168.1.1. What helps it discover the local MAC address?

ARP resolves an IPv4 address to a MAC address on the local network.
CLUE · 03

The destination is outside the laptop's local network. Where does the traffic normally go first?

The default gateway is normally the router that forwards traffic toward other networks.
CLUE · 04

The laptop uses a private address: 192.168.1.24:51520. The router sends the connection outward as: 203.0.113.10:40001. What is happening?

NAT/PAT can translate a private source IP and port into a public IP and port while tracking the connection.
CLUE · 05

The server is listening on port 443. Which service is commonly associated with it?

TCP port 443 is conventionally associated with HTTPS. Port numbers are conventions, not absolute guarantees.
