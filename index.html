ABOUT KSCYBSEC

Curiosity is a good place to start.

KSCybSec is a cybersecurity knowledge space built around a simple idea: cybersecurity becomes easier to understand when complicated ideas are broken into questions, clues and connections.

From networking and web security to offensive and defensive security, OSINT, forensics, malware and beyond, the goal is to explore the field one concept at a time.

You don't need to know everything. Just start with one question.
