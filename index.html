05 / ABOUT

A learning lab for cybersecurity.

KSCybSec is an independent cybersecurity learning space focused on making technical concepts easier to understand.

The goal is simple: explain the idea first, show how it works, and then give you enough practical context to explore it yourself.

From OSINT and SOC fundamentals to networking, Linux and Windows, the focus is on useful knowledge rather than unnecessary complexity.
