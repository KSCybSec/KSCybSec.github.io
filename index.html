05 / ABOUT KSCYBSEC

A place to understand things.

KSCybSec is an independent cybersecurity learning space built around one simple idea: difficult concepts become easier when they're explained clearly.

Here you'll find practical guides, investigations, technical concepts and hands-on learning material covering different areas of cybersecurity.

No unnecessary jargon. No pretending everything is complicated. Just concepts, questions, experiments and things worth understanding.
